Frequently Asked

AI receptionists, answered

What is Kura?

Kura is the Polymathics CRM and agent orchestration platform. It's the underlying system that powers the AI receptionist — managing patient records, conversation context, integrations, and the broader roster of AI agents we deploy across regenerative practices. The voice receptionist is the first agent that runs on Kura, with more on the way.

Is the AI receptionist HIPAA-compliant?

Yes. Voice, transcripts, storage, and downstream integrations all run on HIPAA-compliant infrastructure with signed BAAs. PHI is handled with the same standards a covered entity would apply internally.

Will patients know they're talking to AI?

We disclose AI use clearly and on-brand. The conversation is natural, fluent, and on-tone — patients overwhelmingly report a smooth experience and prefer it to voicemail or hold.

What if a call needs a human?

The AI handles routine intake and booking, but smart escalation rules instantly route urgent or complex situations to your team — via warm transfer, SMS alert, or scheduled callback.

Does it integrate with my booking and EMR?

Yes. We integrate with most modern booking systems and EMRs — or with your CRM as an intermediate layer when direct EMR integration isn't desired.

Can it handle Spanish or other languages?

Yes. The AI is multilingual and can be configured to greet, qualify, and book in additional languages based on your patient population.
