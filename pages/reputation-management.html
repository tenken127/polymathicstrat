Frequently Asked

Reputation, answered

Is automated review collection allowed by Google?

Yes. Asking every patient for an honest review — without filtering or incentivizing — is fully within Google's guidelines. We build systems that comply with Google's review policies and HIPAA simultaneously.

What happens if a patient is unhappy?

Our system catches unhappy patients privately first, routing them to your team for resolution before they ever see a public review form. This protects your average rating and gives you the chance to make things right.

Can you also handle review responses?

Yes. We offer AI-assisted review responses tuned to your tone, with full HIPAA awareness so we never reference protected health information in any public reply.

Which platforms do you cover?

Google is the priority for most regenerative practices, but we also support Yelp, Healthgrades, RealSelf, and other industry-relevant platforms based on your specialty.

How long until I see new reviews?

Most practices see meaningful new review volume within the first 2 weeks of launch, with steady compounding over the following months as your rating climbs.
