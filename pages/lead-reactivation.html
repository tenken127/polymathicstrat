Frequently Asked

Lead reactivation, answered

Is patient outreach actually HIPAA-compliant?

Yes. Every part of our reactivation system — database access, messaging, storage, and AI processing — runs through HIPAA-compliant infrastructure with signed BAAs in place. We treat PHI like the protected asset it is.

Do I need to switch EMRs to use this?

No. We integrate with your existing systems via secure exports, API connections, or HIPAA-compliant CRMs. Most practices keep their EMR exactly as-is.

What kind of results should I expect?

Results vary by database size and treatment mix, but most regenerative practices see meaningful booked appointments within the first 2-3 weeks of launch. We share full reporting on opens, replies, and bookings.

Will it sound robotic to my patients?

No. We tune every message sequence to your tone of voice, and the conversational AI handles two-way replies in a natural, on-brand way — with handoff to your team for anything sensitive.

How do you handle opt-outs and consent?

Every campaign respects opt-out preferences, prior consent records, and TCPA / state-specific guidelines. Opt-outs are honored automatically and immediately.
