Frequently Asked

Paid ads, answered

What's the minimum ad budget you work with?

We're transparent about budget thresholds during your audit. For most regenerative practices, an initial monthly media budget of a few thousand dollars is enough to learn quickly and start scaling profitable campaigns.

Do you handle creative production?

Yes. We produce ad creative specifically tuned to regenerative-medicine patient archetypes — including conversion-driven copy, video scripts, and visuals.

Is paid healthcare advertising HIPAA-compliant?

Yes — when done correctly. We use HIPAA-aware tracking, server-side conversions where required, and strict practices around any PHI handling on landing pages and CRMs.

How is ROI tracked?

We track from click to booked consult to procedure where possible, integrating with your CRM and EMR so reporting shows real revenue, not vanity metrics.

How long until results show up?

Most practices see first booked consults within the first 1–3 weeks of launch, with cost-per-consult improving steadily over the first 60 days as we optimize.
